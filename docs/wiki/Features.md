# Features Overview

**comboom.sucht menu** is packed with features designed to enhance your roleplay and administration experience.

## 🏎️ Race Creator & System

A fully synchronized multiplayer race system built directly into the menu.

- **Create Races**: Build custom tracks on the fly.
  - _Workflow_: Menu -> Race Options -> Race Creator -> Add Checkpoints.
  - Visual checkpoint editing with yellow blips.
- **Save & Load**: Save your favorite tracks to the server (`cbps_races.json`) and load them anytime.
- **Multiplayer**: Races are visible to all players. Join a lobby, wait for the countdown, and race!
- **Leaderboards**: Track finish times and positions.

## 🎙️ Voice & Radio (pma-voice)

Integrated control over your communication settings.

- **Voice Range**: Cycle between Whisper (3m), Normal (10m), and Shout (25m).
- **Radio Channels**:
  - Join channels 1-10 with a single click.
  - Set custom frequencies (up to 999.9).
  - Adjust radio volume directly from the menu.
  - _Requires `pma-radio`._

## 👥 Online Players & Administration

Manage your server's population with ease.

- **Player List**: View all active players.
- **Moderation Tools**:
  - **Teleport**: Go to player or bring them to you.
  - **Spectate**: Watch a player remotely.
  - **Kick/Ban**: Remove disruptive players (ACE secured).
- **Team Management**: Form teams with friends, see teammates on the map, and track standard team colors.

## 🛠️ Player Options

Personal tools for your character.

- **Healing**: Restore health and armor.
- **Cleaner**: Clean player clothes.
- **Appearance**: Change skins/models (if enabled).

## 🚗 Vehicle Options

- **Spawner**: categorized vehicle spawner.
- **Management**: Repair, clean, and delete vehicles.
- **Tuning**: Basic performance upgrades.

## 🔫 Weapon Options

- **Spawner**: Give yourself weapons.
- **Attachments**: Add components to your weapons.
- **Ammo**: Refill ammo.
